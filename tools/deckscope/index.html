How it works. Tempo comes from a spectral-flux onset curve, scored by autocorrelation with a prior centred on club tempos, then refined by fitting a beat grid across the whole track. Key uses a chromagram matched against Krumhansl–Kessler profiles. Loudness follows ITU-R BS.1770 (K-weighting, 400 ms blocks, absolute and relative gates); true peak is a 4× interpolated estimate. Sections come from per-bar energy in the low, mid and high bands.

Supports any format your browser can decode: WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC/M4A, FLAC, OGG. Long files take a few seconds.
